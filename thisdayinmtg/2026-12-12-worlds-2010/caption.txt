16 years ago today, two friends named Guillaume played the Worlds final. 🇫🇷

Guillaume Matignon and Guillaume Wafo-Tapa built their blue-black control deck together and both rode it to the top 8 in Chiba, Japan. On December 12, 2010, Matignon won the mirror 3–1.

The title also tied him with Brad Nelson for Player of the Year. Nelson won the playoff a few months later.
