Three damage. One red mana. 33 years. ⚡

Lightning Bolt was in Alpha in 1993, disappeared from core sets for 14 years after Fourth Edition, and came roaring back in Magic 2010.

Scryfall now lists 72 printings of it, including a brand new one in Secrets of Strixhaven this year.
