28 years ago this week, the DCI fired the first shots of Combo Winter. ❄️

Urza's Saga had turned Standard into a race to combo off first. The December 1, 1998 banned list took out Tolarian Academy and Windfall in Standard and Extended, and restricted Stroke of Genius in Vintage.

It wasn't enough. Six more cards went in March, then Memory Jar was emergency-banned two weeks later.
