Merry Christmas! 🎄 Here are Magic's secret Christmas cards.

Every December since 2006, Wizards of the Coast has handed out a foil holiday card at its holiday party. You can't open them in a pack, and they're full of puns: Fruitcake Elemental, Evil Presents, Stocking Tiger, Yule Ooze.

This year marks 20 years of them.
