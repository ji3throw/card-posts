33 years ago today, Magic went Unlimited. 🤍

On December 1, 1993, Wizards released the game's third printing: the same 302 cards as Beta, a print run of 35 million, and a brand new white border.

Why white? To protect the value of the black-bordered Limited printings that had already sold out. The designers wanted grey, but it was too hard to print.

It even kept Beta's typos. Sorry, Douglas Shuler. 😅
