2027 is the year planeswalkers turn 20. ✨

Lorwyn brought the first five in October 2007: Ajani, Jace, Liliana, Chandra and Garruk. Today there are more than 300 planeswalker cards in paper.

Along the way came the first planeswalker ban (Jace, the Mind Sculptor, 2011) and a set with one in every pack (War of the Spark, 2019).
