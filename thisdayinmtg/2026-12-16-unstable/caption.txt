9 years ago this month, Magic got Unstable. 🐿️

On December 8, 2017, Wizards released its third silver-bordered Un-set: "216-ish" cards of Contraptions, dice rolls, squirrels and jokes you could finally draft on their own.

Packs hid a full-art basic land in every slot, except the 1 in 121 that turned into Steamflogger Boss.
