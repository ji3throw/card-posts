33 years ago today, Magic got its first expansion. 🌙

On December 17, 1993, Arabian Nights hit shelves: 78 unique cards, 8-card boosters and the game's first expansion symbol, a scimitar.

It gave us Bazaar of Baghdad, Library of Alexandria, Juzám Djinn and the first coin flips.
