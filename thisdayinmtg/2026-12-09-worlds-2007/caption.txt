19 years ago today, Israel got its first Pro Tour champion. 🏆

On December 9, 2007, Uri Peleg beat Patrick Chapin 3–1 in the World Championship final in New York, at the end of a four-day event with 386 players from 61 countries.

Bad luck for Chapin: seven years later he made the final again, and lost again to an Israeli player. You saw that one in Monday's reel.
